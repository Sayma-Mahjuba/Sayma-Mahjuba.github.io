About

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) in 2022 and currently live in Ames, Iowa. Before moving to the US, I worked for three years as a Software Quality Assurance (SQA) Engineer at Pathao Ltd. in Dhaka, and earlier completed a functional testing internship at a1qa.

At Pathao, most of my time was spent testing production web, mobile, and API releases across multiple services. Seeing how much manual effort still goes into regression testing, reproducing edge-case bugs, and keeping test suites updated after code changes is what got me interested in graduate research. I want to study how Large Language Models (LLMs) and automated testing tools can help generate useful test cases and make software quality assurance less repetitive and more reliable.

I am applying for Ph.D. programs in Computer Science (Fall 2027) with a focus on Software Engineering and AI-assisted software testing.

Research Interests

  • LLM-based test generation: generating unit, API, and functional test cases with meaningful assertions.
  • Automated software testing: reducing manual overhead in regression and end-to-end web/mobile testing (Playwright, Selenium).
  • AI for Software Engineering: applying NLP and transformer models to practical software quality and defect detection problems.
