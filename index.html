About & Research Vision

I am a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), currently residing in Ames, Iowa, USA. Before relocating, I worked for three years as a Software Quality Assurance (SQA) Engineer at Pathao Ltd.—following a functional testing internship at a1qa and an industrial attachment at Brain Station 23.

Testing multi-service web, mobile, and API workflows in production showed me firsthand how brittle manual regression suites and complex edge cases slow down reliable software delivery. That engineering experience directly motivates my doctoral research goal: investigating how Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI techniques can automate test case generation, strengthen test reliability, and bring scalable intelligence to software quality assurance.
